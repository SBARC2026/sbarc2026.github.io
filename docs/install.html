Install the SBARC app

Management dashboards on your phone and laptop, with the SBARC icon and offline access.

Steps for your device

Open the SBARC app link you received, then follow the steps for your device. You only do this once.

iPhone · iPad

  1. Open the link in Safari.
  2. Tap Share (square with an arrow).
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

Android

  1. Open the link in Chrome.
  2. Tap Install app under the login box, or menu ⋮ → Install app.
  3. Confirm Install.

Windows · Mac

  1. Open the link in Edge or Chrome.
  2. Click Install app under the login box, or the install icon at the right end of the address bar.
  3. SBARC opens in its own window and is added to Start / the Dock.

Using the app

  1. Tap the SBARC icon. Type the project name SBARC and the password you were given.
  2. Switch between Executive Summary and Management Tracking at the top.
  3. To send your weekly sheet, tap Send update, add your Excel file and choose Send with Outlook (laptop) or Other email app / phone.
  4. Tap Lock when you hand the device to someone else.
The app works without internet after the first time you open it. Connect to the company network now and then to get the latest figures.
